Mohembo Bridge Development Impact

14:02 UTC+2
TF
Thabologo Furniture
TATI_EAST · BCP
15th contribution this session
View profile →

Full Question Text

Hon. Furniture asked the Minister of Environment and Tourism:

"Given the strategic importance of the Mohembo Bridge as a critical transport link for communities in the Okavango Delta region, can the Minister provide:

  1. The projected completion timeline for the Mohembo Bridge development project?
  2. The total budgetary allocation for the current financial year and any cost overruns incurred to date?
  3. What specific measures are being implemented to mitigate the impact of construction on local community livelihoods, particularly for those dependent on ferry services?"

Minister's Response

Hon. Minister (Environment & Tourism) responded that the Mohembo Bridge project is in its final phase with projected completion by Q2 2027. Budget allocation for FY 2026/27 is P45 million. A community liaison committee has been established to address livelihood concerns during construction. (Source: Notice Paper No. 14 of 2026)
